## Why
The book ladder leaves its longest (highest-EV) rungs empty. 2026 R21 filled
41 of 48 rungs; every miss was at the $8.00 or $15.00 band. R20 was worse.

This is NOT a band-window problem — 9 to 123 in-window combos existed per
game. It was caused by FIX-NO-PLAYER-DOUBLE-UPS (commit 200b47b), which
banned a player from more than one rung, combined with `search_market_sgms`
filling bands short-to-long.

The arithmetic: 6 rungs x 3 legs needs 18 DISTINCT priced players. A real
Sportsbet/PointsBet scrape covers ~18-25 players per game. The first five
rungs consumed 15 players, so the $15.00 band starved every single time.
A hard one-appearance rule on the book ladder is infeasible by arithmetic,
not by bad luck. That earlier fix's "if a band cannot be filled without
reusing a player, that band is NO BET" is the line being overturned here —
it made starvation the default outcome, not the rare one.

## Change — `search_market_sgms` in `afl_bot/build/report.py` ONLY
- Fill bands **longest-first** (iterate `sorted(target_odds, reverse=True)`).
  The long bands have the thinnest in-window candidate pool, so whichever end
  is filled last is the end that starves. Fill the scarce end while the
  player pool is still untouched.
- Replace the hard one-appearance ban with a COUNT cap: new config constant
  `MAX_RUNGS_PER_PLAYER = 2` in `afl_bot/config.py`. A player may appear in at
  most 2 of the 6 rungs.
- `is_top` (the VALUE PICK promotion) must key off the band VALUE
  (`target == target_odds[-1]`), not the loop index — the loop order changed.
- Output order is UNCHANGED: safest -> longest. The existing sort at the end
  of the function handles this; do not remove it.
- A player still cannot appear twice INSIDE one rung. Unchanged.
- Keep every other selection rule exactly as-is: band window
  (`BAND_UPPER_FACTOR`), best Total EV, disposals preference, tackles/marks
  cap, CHECK PRICING guard, corr_gain haircut, market shrink.

## Do NOT change
- `search_match_sgms` (the MODEL ladder). Its pool is unpriced and never
  starves — it filled 54/54 in R21. It keeps the hard one-appearance rule.
- Anything in `afl_bot/backtest/`. Nothing there calls `search_market_sgms`.
- `BAND_UPPER_FACTOR`. Widening it would let a rung labelled $15 print $24;
  that defeats FIX-RESTORE-BANDS. Leave it at 1.30.
