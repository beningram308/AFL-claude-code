## PART A — BAND SELECTION: PREFER BETTABLE COMBOS, NOT NEAR-LOCKS

**Problem (live R17):** each ladder band picks the SAFEST combo at that price
(highest joint prob). At the book's real prices those are its shortest-priced
near-locks — reliably −EV after margin — so whole ladders read NO BET even when
other combos in the same band have positive Total EV (e.g. West Coast game:
every Sportsbet band NO BET at −9% to −33%, while stakeable combos exist).

**Fix — selection metric change inside each band:**
1. Among candidates whose odds land in the band window (book combo when priced,
   fair otherwise — unchanged), select the combo with the **highest
   market-shrunk Total EV** (the SAME number staking uses: shrunk edge +
   `p_one' * BONUS_BET_FACTOR` promo term). Tie-break: higher joint prob.
2. If the best available Total EV in a band is still <= 0, show the current
   safest combo with NO BET — an honest "nothing worth betting at this price".
   Never hide the band.
3. **Selection-bias guard (project lesson — do not skip):** the selection
   metric must ONLY ever use market-shrunk probabilities (never raw model EV),
   and the existing 15% edge cap + VALUE gating stay. Picking-by-EV amplifies
   the optimizer's curse; the shrink is what keeps it bounded. Add a comment
   citing Phase 3.5 in the code.
4. Model ladder (fair odds, no book prices): unchanged — there's no market EV
   to select on without prices.
5. Tests: band with one −EV near-lock + one +EV alternative at same odds →
   alternative selected, units > 0; band with only −EV combos → safest shown,
   NO BET; selection metric provably uses shrunk probs (unit test on inputs).

**Acceptance A:** regenerated R17 Sportsbet ladders show stakeable rungs
(units > 0) wherever any combo in the band clears Total EV > 0; NO BET remains
only on genuinely dead bands.
