## The sizing model

For a promo-qualified multi (stake-back-as-bonus-bet if exactly one leg misses),
per unit stake f of bankroll, the three outcomes from the SIM'S OWN per-iteration
masks (not naive products):

  - all legs hit          p_win        → +f·(book_odds − 1)
  - exactly one leg miss  p_one_miss   → −f·(1 − BONUS_BET_FACTOR)   # stake back as bonus worth 0.75
  - two+ legs miss        p_bust       → −f

Full Kelly f* = argmax of E[log(1 + outcome)] over those three outcomes — solve
numerically (scipy or a simple bounded golden-section; no closed form needed).
Then apply the EXISTING pipeline unchanged: × KELLY_FRACTION (0.25), per-bet cap,
UNIT_STEP rounding, UNIT_MAX / UNIT_MAX_LONGSHOT, round governor (promo bets now
scale with the round cap like everything else — they're Kelly bets now).
